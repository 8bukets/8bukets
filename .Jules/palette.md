## 2026-01-15 - CLI Visual Feedback
**Learning:** CLI tools often lack visual hierarchy, making it hard to scan for success/failure or key metrics. Adding simple colors and ASCII charts dramatically improves the "glanceability" of the output.
**Action:** Always consider how to make terminal output visually distinct and informative without being overwhelming. Use colors to denote status (Green=Success, Red=Error) and charts for data density.
