## 2025-12-13 - Visualizing Data in Plain Text
**Learning:** Users find raw data tables in CLI/Markdown reports hard to scan. Adding simple ASCII visualization (like bar charts) significantly improves the ability to grasp relative magnitudes at a glance, transforming a dry log into an actionable report.
**Action:** Always consider adding visual indicators (emojis, ASCII charts) to text-based outputs to improve cognitive processing speed.
