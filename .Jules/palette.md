## 2026-01-14 - Text-Based Reports need Visuals
**Learning:** Text-heavy Markdown reports are hard to scan. Users benefit significantly from simple ASCII visualizations (like bar charts) to understand data distribution at a glance.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to add "Distribution" columns with ASCII bars (`█`) in data tables within text/markdown reports.

## 2026-01-14 - Descriptive Links in Tables
**Learning:** Tables with a generic "View" or "Link" column are poor for accessibility and scanability.
**Action:** When possible, make the primary identifier (e.g., Title) the link itself, removing the need for a redundant and non-descriptive link column.
