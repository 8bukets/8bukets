## 2026-01-12 - [ASCII Bar Charts in CLI Reports]
**Learning:** Adding simple ASCII bar charts (`█`) to Markdown tables significantly improves the scannability of numerical data in CLI-generated reports, acting as a low-tech "dashboard" without requiring external visualization libraries.
**Action:** When working on data-heavy CLI tools or reports, look for opportunities to add "Distribution" columns with ASCII visualizations to provide immediate visual context for the data.
