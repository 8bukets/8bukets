## 2026-01-20 - [Enhanced Markdown Report Readability]
**Learning:** Generated Markdown reports often become "walls of text" that are hard to navigate. Users struggle to find specific sections (like "Authors" or "Categories") without scrolling extensively.
**Action:** Always include a Table of Contents (TOC) with anchor links and "Back to Top" links in generated reports. Use emojis to break up visual monotony and comma-separate large numbers for instant readability.
