# Stripe Link for Agents

Give your agent a wallet you control. Let your agents spend on your behalf. Your payment credentials are never exposed. You approve every purchase. Currently available in the US, coming soon globally.

## How to get started

Point your agent to the `skill.md` file, authenticate and grant access.

Tell your agent to:
> Read link.com/skill.md and get me set up with Link

Or run:
```bash
npm install -g @stripe/link-cli
link-cli --help
```

Works with Claude, OpenClaw, and Custom agents.

## How it works

* **Real-time notifications**: Receive an alert when your agent is ready to make a purchase.
* **Transaction approvals**: Stay in control of agent spending by confirming requests in the Link app.
* **Available where you buy**: Agents can purchase using one-time-use cards or shared payment tokens on businesses across the internet.
* **Purchase history**: Track your agent's activity so you can stay on top of all your purchases.

## Coming soon
