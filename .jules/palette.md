## 2026-01-25 - Navigation in Long-form Generated Reports
**Learning:** Generated markdown reports often lack inherent navigation, making them difficult to consume as they grow in length. Users struggle to jump between sections without a Table of Contents or "Back to Top" links.
**Action:** Always include a Table of Contents with explicit HTML anchors (`<a id='...'></a>`) and "Back to Top" links in generated markdown reports longer than one page to improve navigability and user experience.
