## 2026-01-24 - [Markdown Report Navigation]
**Learning:** Long-form Markdown reports are difficult to navigate without explicit structure. Users struggle to jump between sections or return to the top.
**Action:** Always include a Table of Contents with explicit HTML anchors (<a id='...'></a>) and 'Back to Top' links in generated Markdown reports to ensure compatibility and usability.
