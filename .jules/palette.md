## 2026-01-29 - Navigating Long-Form Generated Reports
**Learning:** Generated Markdown reports that exceed a few pages in length become difficult to scan and navigate without explicit structural aids. Plain headers are insufficient for quick jumping between sections, and some Markdown viewers do not auto-generate consistent anchors.
**Action:** Always include a Table of Contents with explicit HTML anchor links (`<a name="..."></a>`) and "Back to Top" navigation in generated long-form content. Use emojis in headers to improve visual scanning speed.
