## 2026-01-04 - [Enhanced Daily Report Formatting]
**Learning:** Even in backend/CLI tools, users appreciate visual hierarchy and scannability. Converting plain text lists to Markdown tables and adding status emojis (✅/❌) makes the "Daily Autonomous Report" much easier to digest at a glance. Small touches like emojis in headers act as quick visual anchors.
**Action:** When generating Markdown reports, always look for tabular data opportunities and use emojis to denote status or categories. Use `.lower()` for robust string comparisons when determining status icons.
