## 2026-01-27 - Navigation & Visual Hierarchy in Text Reports
**Learning:** Generated Markdown reports function as micro-websites. Adding a Table of Contents and "Back to Top" links significantly improves navigability for content exceeding one screen. Emojis in headers enhance visual scanning but necessitate careful handling of anchor links to ensure compatibility (e.g., relying on text-only slug conventions or explicit anchors).
**Action:** Always include a Table of Contents and verify anchor link functionality when generating structured Markdown reports.
## 2026-02-05 - ASCII Visualization in Text Reports
**Learning:** Text-based reports (`REPORT.md`) are often skimmed. Adding simple ASCII bar charts provides immediate visual weight to the data, allowing users to spot trends (like distribution) without parsing numbers.
**Action:** When generating text or markdown reports, always look for opportunities to visualize quantities with simple character-based charts.
