## 2025-12-26 - Robust Reporting
**Learning:** Text-based reports (Markdown) can be significantly improved for UX by adding visual indicators (emojis) and summary tables at the top ("Dashboards"). This provides immediate "at-a-glance" status for users.
**Action:** When designing text reports or CLI outputs, always include a summary section with visual cues (🟢, 🟡, 🔴) for key metrics.
