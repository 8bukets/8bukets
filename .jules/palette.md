## 2026-02-05 - ASCII Visualizations in Text Reports
**Learning:** Adding simple ASCII bar charts (using block characters like █ and ░) to text-based Markdown reports significantly improves data scannability. It allows users to visually compare values in tables instantly without needing to parse the numbers mentally, enhancing the "at-a-glance" utility of the report.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to add "micro-visualizations" to CLI outputs or generated text files (logs, reports) to make density data more accessible.
