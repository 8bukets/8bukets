## 2026-01-23 - Applying UX to Backend Reports
**Learning:** Backend tools often neglect the "reader experience" of their output. Adding semantic emojis and visual status indicators (✅/❌) to generated Markdown reports significantly improves scannability and delight without changing the underlying logic.
**Action:** When working on CLI or automation tools, always look for opportunities to structure the output (logs, reports) using visual hierarchy and symbols to make it human-friendly.
