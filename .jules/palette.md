## 2026-02-01 - [Report Navigation & Data Visibility]
**Learning:** Static Markdown reports act as UIs. Lack of navigation in long reports causes friction. 'N/A' placeholders for available data (dates) break trust in the tool.
**Action:** Always include a Table of Contents and 'Back to Top' links in generated reports. Ensure data parsers have fallbacks (e.g., date vs datetime) to prevent 'N/A' states.
