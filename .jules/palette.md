## 2026-01-22 - [Enhancing Report Scannability with TOC and Emojis]
**Learning:** Adding semantic emojis (e.g., 📊, 🌐) alongside a Table of Contents significantly improves the "scan-and-skip" experience for text-heavy reports. Users can instantly recognize section types without reading the full header.
**Action:** When generating reports, always pair emojis with section titles and ensure anchor links are manually defined to prevent auto-generation issues with special characters.
