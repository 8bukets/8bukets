# First 10 Software Profiles

## Goal

Define a realistic first batch of software profiles for the initial seeded version of the platform.

## Selection Principle

The first software set should feel:

- credible
- useful
- category-diverse
- aligned with existing site themes
- likely to support genuine reviews

## Suggested First 10

### 1. Ahrefs

Category:

- SEO and marketing tools

Why it fits:

- strong buyer intent
- familiar comparison target
- easy to seed balanced reviews around feature depth, data quality, and pricing

### 2. SEMrush

Category:

- SEO and marketing tools

Why it fits:

- natural counterpart to Ahrefs
- supports comparison-driven content and reviews

### 3. Google Analytics 4

Category:

- analytics

Why it fits:

- highly recognizable
- strong usage and opinion diversity
- useful for both positive and critical review angles

### 4. HubSpot

Category:

- CRM and marketing

Why it fits:

- broad commercial relevance
- supports reviews from different business sizes

### 5. WordPress

Category:

- CMS and website platforms

Why it fits:

- already strongly connected to the current domain history
- easy to position with real strengths and tradeoffs

### 6. Webflow

Category:

- CMS and website platforms

Why it fits:

- modern site-building angle
- good contrast against WordPress

### 7. Notion

Category:

- productivity and knowledge management

Why it fits:

- widely recognized
- easy to gather practical workflow-based reviews

### 8. ChatGPT

Category:

- AI productivity tools

Why it fits:

- obvious AI relevance
- strong usage variety
- useful as a trust-oriented review target with nuanced pros and cons

### 9. Vercel

Category:

- hosting and deployment

Why it fits:

- good fit for developer and product audiences
- practical deployment-oriented review angles

### 10. Shopify

Category:

- ecommerce enablement

Why it fits:

- strong business intent
- broad recognition
- realistic mix of positive and critical review perspectives

## Suggested Seed Shape

For the first 10 profiles, aim for:

- 10 software entries fully created
- 8 to 12 total approved reviews
- at least 4 categories represented
- 2 to 4 software pages with deeper early content

## Best Early “Anchor” Pages

If you want a stronger initial impression, make these pages deeper first:

- Ahrefs
- SEMrush
- WordPress
- ChatGPT

These are strong candidates for:

- better descriptions
- more nuanced reviews
- stronger category positioning

## Review Angle Suggestions

Seed reviews should focus on:

- actual use case
- pricing perception
- learning curve
- strengths
- weaknesses
- ideal customer profile

## What To Avoid

- picking only trendy AI tools
- picking only developer products
- picking software with no clear review angle
- choosing obscure tools before major intent categories are covered

## Success Criteria

The first batch is good enough when:

- category coverage feels intentional
- pages do not feel empty
- at least a few profiles have credible review depth
- the platform looks like a real review product, not a skeleton
