# Investor Pitch

## One-Line Pitch

Software Review Platform is a trust-first review platform for software products that uses structured moderation and future AI credibility signals to make software reviews more reliable.

## Problem

Online software reviews are often noisy, manipulated, and difficult to trust.

Buyers make decisions based on weak signals, while software vendors compete in environments where credibility is inconsistent and quality is hard to prove.

## Solution

We are building a software review platform focused on trust by default.

The platform combines:

- authenticated users
- structured review flows
- admin moderation
- comments and ratings
- future AI-assisted review analysis
- future credibility layers such as verified reviewers and trust scoring

## Product

The MVP includes:

- software listings
- user registration and login
- review submission
- moderation workflow
- review comments
- review ratings

This gives us a real trust and discovery foundation before expanding into vendor and monetization features.

## Why Now

Software decisions are increasingly driven by online research, but trust in review platforms remains weak.

At the same time, AI creates both the problem and the opportunity:

- more synthetic content makes trust harder
- better moderation and trust tooling makes trust products more valuable

## Market Logic

The product sits at the intersection of:

- software discovery
- review infrastructure
- trust and moderation
- SaaS buyer intent

That creates room for both traffic-driven and subscription-driven growth.

## Business Model

Potential monetization paths:

- premium vendor listings
- subscriptions for vendor tools
- promotional placements
- sponsored visibility
- future trust or analytics tooling

## Why This Can Win

- trust-first positioning
- structured moderation workflow
- clean product scope for MVP
- extensible architecture for vendor and trust features
- credible path from review utility to monetization

## Current State

Current repo foundation includes:

- Next.js frontend
- Express backend
- PostgreSQL schema
- JWT auth
- moderation flow
- comments and ratings
- Docker-based local setup

## 5-Minute Pitch Script

### 1. Opening

We are building a trust-first software review platform for users who need better signals before choosing software.

### 2. Problem

Today, software reviews are easy to manipulate, hard to verify, and often too shallow to support meaningful buying decisions.

### 3. Solution

Our product introduces a moderated, structured review system with stronger credibility signals and a clear path toward AI-assisted trust scoring.

### 4. Product

Users can browse software, submit reviews, comment, rate, and interact with a platform designed around quality rather than volume.

### 5. Why It Matters

If software reviews become more trustworthy, users make better decisions and vendors benefit from a more credible marketplace.

### 6. Business Model

We can monetize through premium listings, subscriptions, featured placements, and vendor-facing tools.

### 7. Ask

We are building the MVP foundation now and looking to turn that into a scalable product with stronger trust systems, distribution, and vendor monetization.
